About In Scribe

Raised in the log business. Built with pride.

Welcome to In Scribe Log Homes, where quality craftsmanship and tradition meet modern innovation. Founded in 2012, In Scribe is a family-owned business led by Travis and Amber, who have been immersed in the log home industry since childhood.

Travis, with years of experience working alongside his father Greg Johnson of Creative Log Homes, brings unmatched expertise and dedication to each build. Whether you’re dreaming of a cozy cabin in the wilderness or an elaborate custom log home, In Scribe works closely with you and your architect to create a design tailored to your needs and budget.

How We Build

The In Scribe build process.

Although we do have some common plans to work off, most of our builds are custom. In order to make the project enjoyable for everyone, we go over everything so there are no surprises along the way.

  2. 1

    Design together

    We work together with you and one of our trusted architects to create the perfect design to suit your needs, and budget.

  3. 2

    Preliminary plan

    A preliminary plan is created, where you can make any changes you may want to see. We all work together and discuss options along the way.

  4. 3

    Permits & pricing

    Once there is a plan, you can apply for permits if needed. This is also a good time to involve an engineer, and any other contractors to get accurate pricing for all the finishing touches.

  5. 4

    Agreement & scheduling

    Once a final plan is completed, we create a simple contract/agreement so we can book you into our build schedule.

  6. 5

    Pre-built in Rocky Mountain House

    Your home is pre-built at our construction site in Rocky Mountain House, AB, where we pride ourselves on high quality log and timber craftsmanship.

  7. 6

    Shipped & installed

    We disassemble, load onto trailers and ship the home to its final destination, where an In Scribe technical supervisor will oversee the home install.

We typically supply the log or timber package only, so it is good to collaborate the finishing work with local contractors where the home is going. We can help assist with this if you wish.
