03 What We Build

Seven ways we work with wood.

  1. We specialize in handcrafted full scribe log homes using the traditional diamond notch technique, ensuring a precise fit and seamless design. Each log home is custom-built with premium-grade wood, offering durability and timeless beauty for generations to come.

  2. Our post and beam construction method combines rustic charm with structural integrity, creating open, spacious interiors. This versatile technique allows for large windows and modern layouts, perfect for any custom design.

  3. We craft stunning timber frame structures that showcase the natural beauty of wood while providing exceptional strength. These structures can stand alone or complement existing designs, adding warmth and elegance to any home.

  4. We design and build custom log gazebos that provide a charming outdoor retreat for any setting. Crafted with care, these structures offer both functionality and style, perfect for enjoying nature in comfort.

  5. Our log stairs and railings are handcrafted to enhance the natural appeal of your home’s interior and exterior spaces. Each piece is custom-made, ensuring it blends seamlessly with your log or timber home.

  6. Our custom log and timber furniture pieces are handcrafted to match your home’s aesthetic, offering unique, one-of-a-kind creations. From tables to bed frames, each piece is made with care, using high-quality wood for lasting beauty.

  7. Our on-site bandsaw mill allows us to provide custom cutting services for all your unique wood needs. We offer precise cutting for beams, boards, and other custom projects, ensuring top quality and accuracy.

04 The Process

From first sketch to the final log, no surprises along the way.

01/ 06
  1. 01

    Design together

    Although we do have some common plans to work off, most of our builds are custom. We work together with you and one of our trusted architects to create the perfect design to suit your needs, and budget.

  2. 02

    Preliminary plan

    A preliminary plan is then created, where you can make any changes you may want to see. We all work together and discuss options along the way.

  3. 03

    Permits & pricing

    Once there is a plan, you can apply for permits if needed. This is also a good time to involve an engineer if required, as well as any other contractors to get accurate pricing for all the finishing touches.

  4. 04

    Agreement & scheduling

    Once a final plan is completed, we create a simple contract/agreement so we can book you into our build schedule.

  5. 05

    Pre-built at our yard

    All of our homes are pre-built at our construction site in Rocky Mountain House, AB, where we pride ourselves on high quality log and timber craftsmanship.

  6. 06

    Shipped & installed

    Once the package is complete, we disassemble, load onto trailers and ship the home to its final destination, where an In Scribe technical supervisor oversees the install.

Good to know: we typically supply the log or timber package only, so it’s good to collaborate the finishing work with local contractors where the home is going. We can help assist with this if you wish.
