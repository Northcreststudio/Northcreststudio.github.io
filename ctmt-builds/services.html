Service 01

New Home Framing

We take a new house from bare foundation to a framed, sheathed shell that’s ready for roofing, windows and mechanical trades. Walls go up square and plumb, so your drywall, cabinets and finishes go on without fighting the frame.

  • Floor systems, joists and subfloor
  • Exterior and interior walls, stairs and openings
  • Roof framing or truss installation and sheathing
  • Built to your drawings and the BC Building Code

Service 02

Multi-Family & Townhomes

Multi-unit framing is about repetition without drift. We keep layouts consistent unit to unit and floor to floor, and plan the work in phases so plumbers, electricians and HVAC can follow right behind us.

  • Townhomes, duplexes, fourplexes and apartments
  • Party walls, fire blocking and draftstopping
  • Phase-by-phase scheduling with your other trades

Commercial Wood Framing

For general contractors running commercial and mixed-use jobs, we bring an organized crew, clear communication and framing that matches the drawings, so inspections pass and the schedule holds.

  • Wood-frame commercial and mixed-use buildings
  • Shear walls, hold-downs and engineered lumber
  • Coordinated with your site super and inspections

Service 04

Roofs, Trusses & Sheathing

Whether your design calls for engineered trusses or a stick-framed roof, we set it straight and true, then sheathe it so the roofer can start right away.

  • Truss setting, bracing and blocking
  • Stick-framed rafters, valleys, dormers and overbuilds
  • Roof and wall sheathing
  • [ADD: crane-assisted truss setting? confirm]

Service 05

Additions & Structural Renovations

Opening up a wall, adding a second storey or building a garage means tying new framing into an old structure. We frame it so the new and old work together, including beams, headers and posts where the plans call for them.

  • Home additions and second storeys
  • Garages, shops and secondary suites
  • Load-bearing wall removal with beams and posts to the engineer’s spec
  • Reframing after damage or rot

Service 06

Interior & Exterior Walls

Straight walls make every trade after us faster. We frame interior partitions, basement walls and exterior walls with the blocking and backing your cabinets, railings and fixtures need already in place.

  • Basement development and interior partitions
  • Exterior walls and structural openings
  • Blocking and backing for cabinets, rails and fixtures
  • Bulkheads, soffits and chases
