FAQ

Framing questions, answered

What does a framing contractor do?

A framing contractor builds the structure of a building in wood: floor systems, walls, roof framing and sheathing, following the stamped drawings. Framing comes after the foundation and before mechanical, drywall and finishing trades.

What does Overview Framing frame?

Overview Framing Ltd frames custom homes, log homes and cabins, timber frames, multi-family buildings and townhomes, roofs and trusses, additions, garages and basements.

What drives the cost of a framing package?

Square footage, number of storeys, roof complexity, trusses versus a stick-framed roof, site access, crane lifts and the schedule. Send your drawings and scope for a price.

Is framing a log home different from a regular house?

Yes. Log walls settle over time as the logs dry, so framed walls, door and window bucks, stairs and roof posts that meet the logs need settling space or adjustable jacks built in. Roofs are often steeper and heavier for snow.
